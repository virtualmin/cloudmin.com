### Naming

Write the product name as **Cloudmin**, with **Cloudmin GPL** for the free edition and **Cloudmin Pro** or **Cloudmin Professional** for the paid edition. Cloudmin is a product of Cloudmin LLC, which also sells Virtualmin Pro. [Virtualmin](https://www.virtualmin.com/) and [Webmin](https://webmin.com/) are separate products.
