### Sales and purchase support

Cloudmin Pro licenses are sold through the [Virtualmin shop](https://www.virtualmin.com/product/cloudmin-professional-monthly-subscription/). If you have a question about an order or need information on reseller discounts, open a [new ticket](https://forum.virtualmin.com/new-message?groupname=staff&title=Sales+and+purchase+support+request) by sending us a private message to discuss.

### Refund and order cancellation

A refund is guaranteed within the first 30 days of purchase, and can be performed after order cancellation. A request to [staff](https://forum.virtualmin.com/new-message?groupname=staff&title=Refund+request) must be filed to process a refund.

### Press and media contacts

Please visit our online [press kit](/press-kit) for logo imagery in scalable formats. For questions, [contact us](mailto:webmasterATvirtualminDOTcom) using the email address in the link.

### Questions and bug reports

Questions and bug reports about Cloudmin GPL, Virtualmin GPL, Webmin, Usermin and the Authentic Theme should generally be brought up in the [Cloudmin category](https://forum.virtualmin.com/c/cloudmin/) of the Virtualmin community forum. Cloudmin Professional and Virtualmin Professional customers may also request support by sending us a [private ticket](https://forum.virtualmin.com/new-message?groupname=staff&body=%7C++SYSTEM+INFORMATION%7C%7C%0A%7C------------------------------%7C-------------------------------%7C%0A%7C++OS+type+and+version++%7C+++++REQUIRED+++++%7C%0A%7C++Webmin+version+++++++++%7C+++++REQUIRED+++++%7C%0A%7C++Cloudmin+version+++++%7C+++++REQUIRED+++++%7C%0A%7C++Related+packages++++++%7C+++SUGGESTED+++%7C%0A%0A%0A%0A). We try to respond to tickets within 24 hours, seven days a week, including holidays. Private ticket support is for bug reports or queries from customers of our Professional products; for our open-source software we keep the forum response time as low as we can, but that support is provided on an as-available basis with no guarantee about response time.
